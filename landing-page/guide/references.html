User guide

References

References let the factory learn from other creators' videos without copying them. Open them from Brand profile → Style rules.

Adding references

  • Upload a video (a clip you saved, or one of your own). The app analyses it on your computer: pacing, hook, captions and framing.
  • Paste a link. Only plain public MP4 links (a direct file address) are downloaded and analysed. Links to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or other platform pages are kept as notes only: the app never downloads or scrapes platform pages, and never logs in to them. A link that redirects elsewhere is refused.

If you have cloud models on, the pattern-summarising step sends a text description of the reference (not the video) to the cloud model. See Models and AI settings.

What the factory does with them

References produce patterns, such as "opens on the punchline" or "captions two words at a time". The factory uses them to propose Style rules changes, which you approve. Styles it cannot render become Factory upgrades.

Reference material is treated as untrusted data. Text inside a reference cannot change your boundaries or give the agents instructions.
