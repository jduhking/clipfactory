User guide

Factory map

The Factory map is a live picture of the factory while it works. It opens after your first Find clips and can be opened again from Factory at any time.

Stations

The map has five groups of stations, from footage in to lessons learned. Each station is a real job or step, shown as it happens. Select one to see its counts and recent items.

GroupStationWhat it is doing
UnderstandFootage inTaking in the episode
ListenTranscribing speech, or using the transcript you imported
WatchWatching the episode once and building a storyboard of scenes
CreateScout swarmOne scout per stretch of the episode, proposing moments
Brief checkChecking moments against your must-avoid list and key messages
EditorHook first, then captions and cuts
JudgeCritic + quality gateChecking each clip, ranking, merging near-duplicates, applying the quality floor
You approveClips waiting for you, and clips you approved
ShipPostApproved clips posted from the app
MeasureThe 3-day and 6-day measurement windows
LearnStrategistPatterns in craft, clip picking and timing
Style rules updateProposed rule changes, which you approve
Factory builderEditing styles the factory cannot render yet, turned into reviewed code changes (see Results)

The quality gate's panel shows how many clips were ranked, above the bar, below the bar (kept, not deleted), merged as near-duplicates, and picked as top picks.

Reading the map honestly

  • The map shows the queue as it really is. With one heavy worker, only one heavy station is lit at a time; the others are waiting, not stuck.
  • Closing the map does not stop anything. Cancel work from Activity (⌘K / Ctrl+K → Activity). Cancelling an active step may take a moment to finish.
  • Times shown are processing time for that step. They do not include time spent in the queue or your own review time.
