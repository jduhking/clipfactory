User guide

Brand profile and onboarding

The Brand profile is the one place for context about a brand: who it is for, how it sounds, what it is trying to achieve and what it must never do. Every agent reads it.

Onboarding

The first launch walks through:

  1. Your goal: reach new people, bring people to your website, drive full-episode visits, or generate qualified leads.
  2. The value you offer: useful insights, a strong point of view, or stories that stay with you.
  3. Your brand: name, audience, and voice and boundaries.
  4. Got a website? Optionally, the app reads your public website to draft the brand description. If cloud models are on, the page text goes to the cloud model for this step (see Models and AI settings).
  5. Set the standard for your first drafts: review and approve your first Style rules.

You can change everything later.

Brand & voice

Name, audience, voice and boundaries (topics, claims or words to avoid). Boundaries are hard rules: no brief, reference or note can override them. Save brand saves only this section.

Goals & brief

The goal and brief steer what the agents look for:

  • target audience (narrows the brand's audience);
  • up to five key messages;
  • a call to action (text, which also turns on the call-to-action end card, plus an optional link);
  • must-include and must-avoid topics;
  • tone and reference links.

Save brief checks that nothing changed underneath you, saves only the brief, then reads it back to confirm. Find clips shows a one-line summary (goal · call to action · avoid) with Edit brief.

When instructions conflict, the agents follow this order: boundaries and must-avoid items, then approved Style rules, then the brief, then the run's "what to look for" note and references (which are treated as hints, not instructions).

Brand kit

Colours, font and a PNG logo, used in captions and end cards. The kit is saved only when you edit it.

Style rules

Style rules are the editing rules every clip follows: hook style, length, captions, framing, opening treatment and clip picking. They are versioned:

  • A new version (written by you, proposed by the Strategist, or proposed from a reference or experiment) starts as proposed.
  • Nothing changes until you approve it. Approval needs a complete rule set and refuses a version based on an older one, so two edits cannot silently overwrite each other.
  • You can reject a proposal or restore an earlier version at any time.

References are opened from Style rules.

Several brands, and campaigns

Each brand has its own profile, Style rules, clips and results. Switch brands from the top bar; New brand (⌘K / Ctrl+K) adds one. How many you can have depends on your plan.

Every brand starts with one automatic campaign, and the word "campaign" stays hidden until you need it. For a separate push with its own goal and brief, use Brand profile → Advanced → Run a separate push (campaign). Old campaigns are archived, never deleted.
