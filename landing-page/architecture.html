Architecture

Heavy work at home.
A small cloud for the rest.

The editing happens on your computer. The cloud only signs you in, posts what you approved and keeps your plan in step with Stripe.

01 / System context

Who talks to whom.

Your library lives on your computer. The app reaches out only for the things that need the internet, and only to the services that do them.

  • On your computer: episodes, transcripts, clips, Style rules and results, plus the local models and FFmpeg.
  • Growth Factory cloud: sign-in, the posting service and billing. It never receives your library.
  • Cloud models are optional: with your own key, agents send text (and still frames only if you allow it).

02 / Containers

What runs where.

The app is one Python engine behind a native window. The cloud is two small functions over a database that only lets each account see its own rows.

  • Window and interface: pywebview hosts one bundled React page. It calls the engine on 127.0.0.1 with a per-launch session.
  • Engine: FastAPI with a job queue that runs one heavy task at a time, so transcription, models and rendering never fight for memory.
  • Secrets stay put: your API keys are in the system keychain; the Zernio key exists only in the posting function's secrets.

03 / Posting

Approved, then posted once.

Posting starts from an approved revision and is built to never double-post, even if the network drops mid-request.

  • Exactly once: each post carries an idempotency key; if the outcome is unknown, the engine reads the post back instead of resending.
  • Never late on its own: schedules run while the app is open. A time missed while it was closed waits for you as overdue.
  • Scoped: a brand only posts to the accounts you connected to it, and the plan check happens on the server.

04 / Learning loop

Every post teaches the next clip.

The stations in the app's Factory map, in order. Two of them are you.

  • Understand and create: Listen and Watch read the episode, the scout swarm proposes moments and the Editor cuts them.
  • Judge: the Critic and quality gate check each clip against your Style rules before you see it.
  • Learn: the Strategist reads measured posts and proposes a Style rules change. It applies only after you approve it.

05 / Billing

Stripe is the source of truth.

The app never handles a card. Stripe tells the billing function what you bought; the app asks the billing function what you may do.

  • Webhooks win: signed Stripe events update one subscription row per workspace. Retries are safe, and an older event never overwrites a newer one.
  • Limits on both sides: the app shows the limits, and the posting function enforces them.
  • Kind failure modes: offline, the plan is kept for 7 days; a downgrade never deletes anything.

Principles

The rules the
design follows.

Mermaid versions of these diagrams, with more detail, are in docs/architecture.md ↗.

  1. 01

    Local first

    Footage and the library stay on your computer. Anything that leaves is named before it goes.

  2. 02

    You approve

    Clips, Style rules changes and factory upgrades all wait for a person.

  3. 03

    Honest numbers

    Scores order clips; they do not predict views. Results are observations, not proof.

  4. 04

    Nothing silent

    Interrupted work is shown, not restarted. Missed schedules wait. Failed cloud calls say so.

  5. 05

    Keys on the server

    Platform and payment secrets live in the cloud functions, never in the app.

  6. 06

    Never delete on downgrade

    Plans limit what you create next, not what you already made.
