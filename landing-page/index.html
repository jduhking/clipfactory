Feature 01 / Agents cut the clips

Agents cut it up.
You keep control.

Drop in a saved stream. Local speech-to-text gives the agents a transcript, they mark the moments worth keeping, and each cut lifts out as a captioned vertical draft.

  • Transcript-backed choices. Every suggested moment shows the lines and timecodes behind it, so you can judge meaning rather than energy.
  • Vertical drafts, not clip dumps. Framing, burned-in captions and audio treatment happen in the same pass.
  • Revisions never overwrite. Ask for a different cut and the earlier one stays in the library.

Ranking orders your review queue; it does not predict views. Rendering runs on your Mac, and local models need disk space, time and suitable hardware.

Feature 02 / Posting

Then it goes out
where you said.

When you approve a revision, the app can hand it to your connected accounts — or you export the file and upload it yourself. Nothing leaves without that approval.

  • Approval is the gate. Approve, export and post stay separate actions; a new revision is not permission to publish it.
  • Several destinations. TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, with Facebook, LinkedIn, X and Threads integrations also in the codebase.
  • Uncertain results reconcile. A post with an unclear outcome is matched by its external id instead of being blindly retried.

Connected posting needs a paid plan, a supported account and live verification, so the first Mac beta stays export-first. This page promises no audience growth.

Feature 03 / Results

Check what
actually worked.

Posted clips come back with whatever the platforms report, next to the source moment and the revision you approved. Your next session starts from what your audience responded to.

  • What was not reported stays blank. A missing metric is shown as unavailable, never as a zero that looks like failure.
  • Review in context. Results sit beside the clip, its moment in the stream and the exact revision you signed off.
  • Collection needs the app open. Scheduling and metric refresh run while Growth Factory is running; there is no cloud worker doing it for you.

Numbers come from the platforms, not from us. Scores order your review queue rather than forecasting performance, and demo data stays labelled and separate from real results.

Before you join.

A few honest answers.

Early access, without the fine-print surprises.

Who is the first beta for?

Small streamers on Apple Silicon Macs who have recorded streams and want help making short clips. We're looking for real footage and honest feedback—not a certain follower count.

Are the animations on this page the app?

No. They are diagrams of the workflow, labelled as animated illustrations, drawn over the same topographic field the app uses. The only screenshot is the app interface above.

What happens after I join the waitlist?

Your email draft reaches us directly. We reply as cohorts open up, starting with Apple Silicon Macs. There is no queue position to check and no automatic acceptance.

Is there a signed Mac installer ready?

Not yet. Join the waitlist above and tell us your Mac model. Developer source setup is documented on the setup page. Public downloads wait for a signed, verified release.

Does it clip my stream while I'm live?

The current workflow starts with a saved recording. Live stream capture, automatic chat-highlight detection and background cloud clipping are not promised.

Does it work without cloud AI keys?

Local speech and local agents can run on your Mac once their models are installed. Setup needs disk space, time and suitable hardware. Optional cloud speech and models use your own keys; provider charges are separate.

Will it post without me reviewing?

No. You review and approve the exact clip revision. Export and posting are separate actions. The initial beta is export-first; connected posting needs a paid plan, a supported account and live verification.

What about Windows, Linux and scheduled posts?

The codebase includes Windows and Linux support, but the first user cohort is Mac-first. Scheduling and metric collection currently require the app open. There is no general cloud sync or shared team workspace.
