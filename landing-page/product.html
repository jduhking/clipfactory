From direction to iteration

Every step carries
the context forward.

A clip is one output. The system keeps the brief, the creative decision, and the evidence that informs what comes next.

    Brand, audience, goals.

  2. 02Create

01 / Brand profile

Start with what
makes it yours.

Bring your website, audience and voice into a shared brief. Keep goals, boundaries and Style rules together, so each production starts with a direction.

Website-assisted drafts show their source text. Inferred suggestions stay separate.

02 / Factory

Put the agents
to work.

Add an episode. Speech, source analysis and scout agents find promising moments. The renderer shapes vertical drafts with openings, captions, framing and audio treatment.

See the actual work and recorded progress in the Factory map. A score helps order the clips; it does not predict views.

03 / Library

Your creative.
Within reach.

Play the clips that were made for your brand. Keep one, request a change, make an edit or remove it. Sources and versions stay connected, so a new draft has a clear starting point.

04 / Results

Give the next move
a reason.

Connect the account and publication to the creative that produced it. Inspect the observations, the proposed change and the next experiment in the same workflow.
