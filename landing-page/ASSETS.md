# Assets

- The alpine photograph was generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this landing page. It is illustrative and does not represent customer footage, real campaign output, or an endorsement. The original is retained as `assets/alpine-story-original.png` (excluded from deployment); `assets/alpine-story.webp` is the optimized full frame.
- `assets/peaks.webp`, `valley.webp`, `hiker.webp`, `hiker-close.webp`, `towers.webp` and `summit.webp` are crops of that same photograph, retained as historical assets; the homepage no longer uses them.
- `assets/growth-factory-logo.png` is an unchanged copy of the user-supplied Mac app logo in `frontend/src/growth/assets/growth-factory-logo.png`. `icon.svg`, `mark.svg`, `mark-white.svg`, and `favicon.svg` embed that artwork, framed identically to the Mac app and displayed in white on a Pearl Studio violet (#a75de3) rounded square with an SVG color filter. No raster artwork was edited.
- `assets/pearl-studio.jpg` and `assets/social-card.jpg` are unchanged copies of the real app screenshot in `docs/images/pearl-studio.png` (the source is JPEG despite its historical extension). It shows illustrative brand data and is used for link previews; the homepage no longer shows it.
- `assets/app-factory.webp` (1440x900) and `assets/app-learn.webp` (882x454) are screenshots of the real desktop app: the Factory screen with Create open, and the Learn stage with rules waiting for approval. They were captured from the frontend preview mode (`?fixtures=flow`), with the preview data renamed for a sample streamer channel: the brand is "Night Owl", the account `@nightowl_plays`, the run ID and clip titles are new, and the "Preview fixture:" prefixes are removed. The layout, labels, stages and counts are the app's own preview data, not a customer's result.
- The two animations on the homepage (`motion.js`) are drawn in HTML and CSS from the app's surfaces; they are not recordings. The scout animation follows `clip_factory/scouts.py` (ten-minute stretches, up to two scored moments per scout, the strongest distinct ones kept) and shows its real progress messages. The loop animation follows `clip_factory/growth_loop.py` (one change per test, equal measurement windows, rules only after approval). The channel, file name, hooks, captions and scores are sample content.
- `assets/streamers/` holds licensed stock photos from Pexels, used under the Pexels licence (https://www.pexels.com/license/: free for commercial use, no attribution required; identifiable people may not be shown in a bad light or as endorsing a product). They are photos of models, not customers or endorsements, and the homepage never presents them as users. All seven are from one series by Tima Miroshnichenko, as ffmpeg crops and WebP re-encodes of the Pexels originals with no person altered: `stream-talk.webp` (1280x720) from https://www.pexels.com/photo/7047347/, `stream-play.webp` (1280x720) from 7047530, `stream-pizza.webp` (1280x720) from 7047345, `stream-moment.webp` (1280x720) and `clip-moment.webp` (720x1280) from 7047236, `clip-play.webp` (540x960) from 7047002, and `clip-talk.webp` (540x960) from 7046699. The hooks and captions drawn over them are the animation's, not words from the photos.
- Type is Instrument Sans Variable, matching the desktop app. The Latin variable WOFF2 is self-hosted from `@fontsource-variable/instrument-sans` 5.3.0. Its SIL Open Font License is included at `assets/fonts/instrument-sans-LICENSE.txt`. No remote font request is needed.

## Image prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: cinematic sample footage still for a premium creative software landing page, landscape 1536x1024. Create one beautiful editorial adventure photograph: a lone small hiker wearing a vivid burnt-orange technical jacket and a pale cap, seen from behind standing on a rocky ridge in the lower right third, looking into a dramatic layered alpine valley in the Italian Dolomites. Craggy limestone peaks, deep pine-green valleys, atmospheric muted sage and grey-green distant mountains, pale creamy sky, soft warm late-afternoon light and very subtle analog 35mm grain. Premium independent outdoor film, real natural texture, considered quiet composition, art directed yet believable. Strong silhouette and terracotta color accent against the rugged olive landscape. Camera perspective from a few meters behind the hiker. Wide generous valley in the center and left, hiker about one fifth of image height. No letters, no logos, no watermarks, no UI, no borders, no extra panels. The output is a single photographic scene.

- `assets/topography.svg` is a static fallback derived from the terrain equation in
  `frontend/src/growth/contours.ts` at t=41, using marching-squares contours. The
  animated hero background uses the original app shader, not generated video.
