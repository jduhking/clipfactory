Questions you should ask first.

Early access without the fine-print surprises.

Who is the first beta for?

Streamers on Apple Silicon Macs with recordings to clip. We’re looking for real footage and honest feedback, not a follower count.

Are the animations on this page the app?

No. They are diagrams of the workflow, labelled as animated illustrations. The only screenshot is the app at the top of the page.

Is there a signed Mac installer?

Not yet. Public downloads wait for a signed, verified release. Developer source setup is documented on the setup page.

Does it clip my stream while I’m live?

No. The workflow starts with a saved recording. Live capture, automatic chat-highlight detection and background cloud clipping are not promised.

Does it work without cloud AI keys?

Local speech and agents run on your Mac once their models are installed, which needs disk space, time and suitable hardware. Cloud models are optional and use your own keys.

What about Windows, Linux and scheduled posts?

The codebase supports Windows and Linux, but the first cohort is Mac only. Scheduling and metric collection need the app open, and there is no shared team workspace.
