One loop, five stations.

Every recording goes round the loop the app draws in its Factory map, and you are the gate before anything ships.

  1. Understand

    It reads the whole stream first.

    Drop in a saved recording. The transcript and scene changes are worked out on your Mac, so every suggestion can point back to the lines it came from.

  2. Create

    Scouts find moments. The Editor cuts them.

    Scout agents find moments worth keeping, and the Editor cuts each into a vertical draft: hook first, bold word by word captions, framed on the speaker. A new cut never replaces the old one.

  3. Judge

    The Critic ranks. You decide.

    Every draft is scored on an eight part production rubric. Ranking decides what you see first, not what will do well. Nothing moves on until you approve it.

  4. Ship

    Approved clips go out to your channels.

    Export the file, or post to a connected TikTok, Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts account. An unclear post is matched by its external id, never blindly retried.

    Connected posting needs a paid plan, a supported account and live verification, so the first beta is export only.

  5. Learn

    What comes back shapes the next batch.

    Whatever each platform reports comes back to the Strategist, which proposes one Style rule at a time for the Editor, like opening on the payoff. You approve a rule before it changes anything.

    A channel that has not reported stays blank, never zero. Metric refresh and scheduling need Growth Factory running.

Loop after loop, it learns your channel.

Each loop leaves the factory knowing more about your audience and your taste. That memory lives in four places you can read and change.

Your taste
Learned from what you approve. It nudges review order a few points and never overrides the rubric.
Beliefs
What seems to be working, with the evidence beside it. Observational, not proof.
Style rules
One approved change at a time, and the next batch is re-cut with it.
Experiments
Drafts that change one thing, and about one pick in four spent exploring.

Real and simulated runs are kept apart, and demo data stays labelled. Nothing here guarantees growth.

Questions you should ask first.

Early access without the fine-print surprises.

Will it grow my channel?

Nothing can promise that. Each loop makes the next batch better informed by what you approved and what came back, and says how sure it is. Growth still depends on your stream.

Are the animations on this page the app?

No. They are diagrams of the loop, labelled as animated illustrations. The two screenshots, the app at the top and the Factory map, are the real interface on sample data.

Is there a signed Mac installer?

Not yet. Public downloads wait for a signed, verified release. Developer source setup is documented on the setup page.

Does it clip my stream while I’m live?

No. The workflow starts with a saved recording. Live capture, automatic chat-highlight detection and background cloud clipping are not promised.

Does it work without cloud AI keys?

Local speech and agents run on your Mac once their models are installed, which needs disk space, time and suitable hardware. Cloud models are optional and use your own keys.

What about Windows, Linux and scheduled posts?

The codebase supports Windows and Linux, but the first cohort is Mac only. Scheduling and metric collection need the app open, and there is no shared team workspace.
