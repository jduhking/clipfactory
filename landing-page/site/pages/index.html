Feature 01 / Factory

Find the moment
worth clipping.

Drop in a saved stream. Local speech-to-text turns hours of footage into a transcript the factory can actually read, so candidate moments arrive with the words that made them good.

  • Start from a recording you already have. Set your voice, topics and caption style once in a brand profile.
  • Read the evidence before you watch. Each suggested moment shows its transcript lines and timecodes.
  • Review in a sensible order. A ranking decides what you look at first.

Ranking orders your review queue. It does not predict views, and scores are not a promise about performance.

Feature 02 / Studio

Cut it vertical.
Keep your voice.

Studio builds the 9:16 draft: vertical framing, burned-in captions and audio treatment in the same graphite workspace as the rest of the app. You watch the real clip, not a mock-up.

  • Captions that match your channel. Caption styling and brand settings carry from clip to clip.
  • Ask for a change, get a new draft. Revisions are added next to the original instead of overwriting it.
  • Check it properly. Speech, framing and timing are all reviewable before anything is approved.

Rendering runs on your machine. Local models need disk space, time and suitable hardware; optional cloud models use your own keys.

Feature 03 / Library

Approve the exact
version. Then export.

Sources and every clip revision stay in one library, so you always know which cut you signed off on. Approve a specific revision, export the file, and upload it wherever your audience is.

  • Nothing posts itself. Approve, export and post are separate actions you take on purpose.
  • Every revision is kept. Go back to the earlier cut whenever the new one is worse.
  • Export-first in this beta. You upload the finished clip to TikTok, Reels or Shorts yourself.

The app also includes connected posting for several platforms, but that path needs a paid plan and live verification, so the first beta stays export-first. Metric collection needs the app open; there is no general cloud sync.

Before you join.

A few honest answers.

Early access, without the fine-print surprises.

Who is the first beta for?

Small streamers on Apple Silicon Macs who have recorded streams and want help making short clips. We're looking for real footage and honest feedback—not a certain follower count.

What happens after I join the waitlist?

Your email draft reaches us directly. We reply as cohorts open up, starting with Apple Silicon Macs. There is no queue position to check and no automatic acceptance.

Is there a signed Mac installer ready?

Not yet. Join the waitlist above and tell us your Mac model. Developer source setup is documented on the setup page. Public downloads wait for a signed, verified release.

Does it clip my stream while I'm live?

The current workflow starts with a saved recording. Live stream capture, automatic chat-highlight detection and background cloud clipping are not promised.

Does it work without cloud AI keys?

Local speech and local agents can run on your Mac once their models are installed. Setup needs disk space, time and suitable hardware. Optional cloud speech and models use your own keys; provider charges are separate.

Will it post without me reviewing?

No. You review and approve the exact clip revision. Export and posting are separate actions. The initial beta is export-first; connected posting needs a paid plan, a supported account and live verification.

What about Windows, Linux and scheduled posts?

The codebase includes Windows and Linux support, but the first user cohort is Mac-first. Scheduling and metric collection currently require the app open. There is no general cloud sync or shared team workspace.
