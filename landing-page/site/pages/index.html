Five steps, every stream.

The same loop every time, and you decide at every step that matters.

  1. Find

    It finds the moments worth sharing.

    Drop in last night’s recording. It listens to what you say, skips the intro, sponsor reads and dead air, and pulls out moments with a hook, one complete idea and a payoff. It all runs on your Mac.

    Why it matters: you never scrub back through a six hour VOD again.

  2. Cut

    Each one is cut vertical, ready to post.

    The crop follows your face, a short hook goes on top, and bold captions land word by word, kept clear of your face. Clips run about 20 to 60 seconds.

    Why it matters: you review a finished clip, not a timeline. No editing skills needed.

  3. Pick

    You keep it or skip it.

    Clips come to you in order, the strongest first. Keep the ones that feel like you and skip the rest. The order decides what you see first, not what will do well.

    Why it matters: nothing posts without you, and what you keep teaches it your taste.

  4. Post

    Kept clips go to TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

    Download the files, or post straight to a connected account. A post that may have failed is checked, never blindly sent twice.

    Why it matters: one stream becomes clips on every short video app you use.

    Connected posting needs a paid plan, a supported account and live verification, so the first beta is export only.

  5. Learn

    It sees what people watched, and suggests one change.

    Each platform reports views and how much people watched. It suggests one change at a time, like opening on the big moment, and nothing changes until you approve it.

    Why it matters: the next batch is shaped by your audience, not by guesswork.

    A platform that has not reported stays blank, never zero. Checking results and scheduling need the app open.

Questions streamers ask first.

Early access without the fine print surprises.

Do I need editing experience?

No. It hands you finished vertical clips with the hook and captions already on, and you decide which to keep. There is no timeline to learn.

Will it grow my channel?

Nothing can promise that. Each stream makes the next batch better informed by what you kept and what people watched, and it says how sure it is. Growth still depends on your stream.

Can I paste a Twitch link?

Not yet. Download your VOD, or use the recording your streaming software saves, and drop in the file.

Does it clip my stream while I’m live?

No. It starts from a saved recording after you finish. Live capture, chat highlight detection and cloud clipping in the background are not promised.

Does my footage leave my Mac?

Not to be cut. Finding moments and cutting run on your Mac once the local models are installed. Cloud models are optional and use your own keys; if you choose them, the transcript and a few frames go to that provider.

Are the people on this page real streamers?

They are licensed stock photos of models, used to show the idea. The animations are illustrations, and the one screenshot is the real app with sample data.

Is there a signed Mac installer?

Not yet. Public downloads wait for a signed, verified release. Developer source setup is documented on the setup page.
