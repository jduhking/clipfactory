Questions

Do I need editing experience?

No. You get finished vertical clips with the hook and captions already on, and you decide which to keep. There is no timeline to learn.

Can I paste a Twitch link?

Not yet. Drop in a saved recording: your VOD download, or the file your streaming software saves.

Do I have to connect my accounts?

No. Download the clips, post them yourself and add the views by hand. Connecting an account lets it post and read results for you, on paid plans.

Does my footage leave my Mac?

Not to be cut. Finding moments and cutting run on your Mac. Cloud models are optional and use your own keys.

Is there a Mac installer?

Not yet. Downloads open once the signed release is ready. Developers can set it up from source on the setup page.
