Billing questions

Straight answers.

How do I upgrade?

Set up the app and use it on Free. When you want more, open Settings → Plan and billing and choose Upgrade to Pro or Upgrade to Business, monthly or yearly. Stripe Checkout opens in your browser; the app picks up the new plan within seconds, without a restart.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Manage billing opens Stripe's billing portal, where you cancel, switch plans, update your card or download invoices. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you paid for.

What happens to my clips if I downgrade?

Nothing is deleted. Every brand, clip and result stays available; only creating more than the new plan allows is blocked. If you have more connected accounts than the plan allows, posting is held until they fit.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The app keeps your plan for up to 7 days from its last successful check, then works as Free until it can check again.

Are AI costs included?

Local models are free to run. Cloud models are optional: you add your own OpenRouter or Groq key and pay the provider directly. The default model costs under 1¢ per read.

Who handles payment?

Stripe. Card details never pass through the app or our servers; we store only your plan, its status and its dates. See the privacy policy.
