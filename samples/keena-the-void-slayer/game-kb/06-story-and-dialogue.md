# 06 Story and dialogue

All quotes are verbatim, with the game's own punctuation and caps. The full transcript is in the game repo at
`docs/agent-kb/13-dialogue-transcript.md`. Levels 2 to 4 have no dialogue.

## Plot in one paragraph

One starry night a meteor falls on the peaceful island of Lepordia. Keena and her little brother Cleo find a silent
void creature in the crater; it takes a liking to her and she names it Moku. Barto the cat knight arrives with news:
shadow creatures are spreading corruption across the land, and even Queen Millie's hive has fallen. Then the shadows
snatch Cleo in a cage. Keena and Moku cross Petunia Plains, Carrot Crossing, Sakura Forest and Solfege Steppe,
purifying the land and gathering Rainbow Prisms, whose light the old legends say can cleanse the Void. At the hive they
find Barto wounded, ride a giant acorn to the queen's door, and fight the corrupted Millie. With every prism Keena
draws the Void out of her; Millie learns to trust Moku and her drones bring Cleo home. Without every prism Millie
stays infected, but she still has her drones bring the boy down.

## Opening cutscene

A comic-panel and staged opening at night (music: Opening Night theme).

1. Narration over a starry night: "One starry night...." / "In a happy go-lucky and peaceful land, Lepordia" / "the
   peace was about to be disrupted ...."
2. A meteor; the clean island map, then the hive and the giant tree before and after corruption.
3. The crater:
   > **Keena:** What in the world was THAT?
   > **Cleo:** Sis... it's... it's... A MONSTER!!!
   > **Keena:** Let me see... Hmmm...
   > **???:** ...
   > **Keena:** I think he takes a liking to me. Do you have a name?
   > **???:** ...
   > **Keena:** No? .... OK, well.... how about MOKU?
4. Barto arrives:
   > **Barto:** Keena! Thank goodness I've found you.
   > **Keena:** Well! If it isn't my favorite raccoon knight!
   > **Barto:** A cat, if ye please. As I have told ye time and— Forgive me. There is no time for this.
   > **Barto:** The island is under attack! ... Creatures of shadow have overrun our lands. Wherever they pass,
   > corruption follows. Even Queen Millie's hive has been corrupted. What is happening to her in there, I cannot say.
5. The kidnapping:
   > **Cleo:** Uhhh, I see something...
   > **Keena:** WHAT THE
   > **Barto:** ...
   > **Keena:** CLEO!!!!

The trailer shows this as Cleo carried off in a birdcage by void creatures with glowing eyes.

## Petunia Plains

The first petunia puzzle: "Hmm... how do I get up there?" / Moku: "Mrrp! Mrrp mrrp!" / "Oh really?"

The prism discovery at the exit gate:

> **Barto:** Hmm… ah! Above you, Keena!
> **Keena:** A rainbow shard? Look, Moku. It's warm!
> **Moku:** Mrrp!
> **Barto:** A Rainbow Prism. The old legends say their light has cleaning properties.
> **Barto:** Bring every shard together, and we might rid the land of this blight.
> **Keena:** Then we'll keep looking. Come on, Moku!

If Keena already has a prism, she says "Another rainbow shard! Just like the one we found, Moku. It's warm too!" and
Barto "So ye've found one already."

## The fight's end and the endings

On the last hit Millie shrieks, the screen wobbles, she falls in slow motion with red pulses and five explosion bursts, and
the screen flashes. Then, depending on the prisms:

**Good ending (all 12 prisms).** The prisms orbit Keena's raised hand and fire into Millie one by one.

> **Keena:** Hang on, Millie. I've got every Prism. Barto said their light could draw out the Void.
> **Millie:** Oh… what happened? The buzzing is gone. ...Keena? Is that you?
> **Millie:** Stay back! That thing reeks of the Void! *(she thrusts her spear at Moku)*
> **Keena:** Millie, wait! This is Moku. He helped me save you.
> **Millie:** ...If you trust him, then so do I.
> **Millie:** Cleo's up above us. My drones are with him.
> **Keena:** Cleo?! Is he okay?
> **Millie:** Drones! Bring him down. Gently!

Cleo's reunion is wordless. The Friendship Theme carries into the credits.

**Incomplete ending (fewer than 12).**

> **Keena:** Millie? Can you hear me?
> **Keena:** She's still infected. What do we do, Moku?
> **Keena:** I can't draw it out. The Prisms' light isn't whole.
> **Millie:** I can still get the child out. Drones… bring him down.

Both end with the reunion, then the credits: the animated logo, the dev-team portraits and a thank-you. Ending art
includes a purified giant tree and a departure scene (`Sprites/Cutscenes/Ending/`).
