# 02 Characters

Looks come from the in-game sprite sheets (paths given, under `Sprites/` in the game repo) and the KTVS DS Style
Moodboard by Vi Nguyen in this project's Knowledge Base. Lines are verbatim from the game.

## Keena

- **Role:** the hero and the player character. "Keena" means courage (devlog).
- **Look:** a small chibi girl. Big pink hair worn up in two **bunny-ear** shapes, large light-green eyes, a teal
  dress with a white collar, dark green boots. In story art she is often in green. Sprite:
  `Sprites/Player/keena_sprite_sheet.png` (64×64 cells, 124 frames).
- **Personality:** brave, impulsive, warm, a bit goofy. She shouts in caps when things go wrong ("WHAT THE",
  "CLEO!!!!") and gets giddy at big moments ("OH WOW, thats a big boi", "That was ... AWESOME!").
- **Gameplay truth:** she "can't really fight on her own — she's small and she knows it". She jumps, dashes,
  wall-jumps and commands Moku.
- Origin: grew out of James sketching animal "hats" (frog, hawk, porcupine) Kirby-style.

## Moku

- **Role:** Keena's companion and her entire moveset. "Moku" is charcoal in Japanese (devlog); Keena names him in
  the opening ("how about MOKU?").
- **Look:** a small, rounded, dark navy-to-teal blob of void goop with **two upright pointed ear-like tips** and two
  small white square eyes. **No limbs.** He floats beside Keena. Sprite: `Sprites/Moku/moku_sprite_sheet.png`
  (75 frames); dialogue faces `moku-neutral/silent/sleep/unamused.png`; ball form `mokuball.png`.
- **Never describe or generate him as a rabbit or bunny.** He has ear points, not a rabbit body. (This was corrected
  in the trailer work: the live-action draft made him a rabbit and had to be redone.)
- **Voice:** silent or "Mrrp! Mrrp mrrp!". Shown as `???` before he is named.
- **Forms in the game:** grows SMALL / MEDIUM / LARGE with absorbed goop; Ball Moku (swallows a giant acorn and rolls).
  The Clone Bomb (six clones) was cut.
- A creature of the Void that sides with Keena; Millie at first says "That thing reeks of the Void!"

## Cleo

- **Role:** Keena's little **brother**. He calls her "Sis". Snatched by shadow creatures in a cage in the opening; the
  rescue is the goal.
- **Look:** short reddish-pink hair, light-blue top and trousers. Sprites: `Sprites/Entities/Cleo/` (idle, walk, cry,
  caged).
- Lines: "Sis... it's... it's... A MONSTER!!!"; "Uhhh, I see something...".
- Reunited with Keena wordlessly in both endings; Millie's drones bring him down.

## Barto (Bartholomew, "Barth")

- **Role:** a knight and Keena's friend, the mentor who explains the stakes.
- **Look:** a blue-grey **cat** with a white muzzle and chest, dark blue tunic and cape, carrying a blade. Sprite:
  `Sprites/Entities/Barto/barto_complete.png` (idle, hurt, walk). His dialogue name tab reads "Bartholomew".
- **Running joke:** Keena: "my favorite raccoon knight!" — Barto: "A cat, if ye please. As I have told ye time
  and—"
- **Voice:** old-fashioned and formal: "ye", "hath", "'gainst", "I know not what happened".
- Found injured outside the hive in Level 5 after facing the corrupted queen.

## Queen Millie

- **Role:** queen of the bee hive, corrupted by the Void; the final boss. Keena knows her and wants to save her,
  not beat her.
- **Look:** a tall bee warrior queen: golden helmet with a curved crest, translucent wings, golden arm and leg armour,
  brown top, orange-brown cape, a long ornate spear. Sprite: `Sprites/Entities/Enemies/Boss/Millie/millie_complete.png`
  (144×144 cells: idle, hurt, swoop, spear swing, slam, pullout, wing flap, seated, purified).
- Commands drones and summons void bees. After she is purified: "Oh… what happened? The buzzing is gone."

## Narrator

Opens the game: "One starry night.... In a happy go-lucky and peaceful land, Lepordia ... the peace was about to be
disrupted ...."

## The Void and the voidlings

"Creatures of shadow have overrun our lands. Wherever they pass, corruption follows." The corruption is purple-black
**void goop**. It drains colour, corrupts plants (wheat, petunias, bamboo) and animals (bunnies), and twists creatures
into the enemies in [04](04-enemies-and-bosses.md). Rainbow Prisms' light is said to cleanse it.

## Design rules for clips

- Keena is small next to everything; keep her readable at 320×180 rather than enlarging her.
- Moku should always be near Keena and floating, with the ear tips visible.
- Barto is a cat; Cleo is a boy; Millie is a victim as much as a boss.
- Dialogue in cutscenes sits *below* the characters, framed so Keena and Moku stay visible (a rule in the game's own
  docs).
